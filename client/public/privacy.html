Let’s Move It privacy

Let’s Move It is made and run by Starside Movers.

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • Your mover name and email, when you clock in. We need these to save your mover, your stats and your spot on the daily leaderboard.
  • Your newsletter preference. Playing the game includes agreeing to newsletters and offers from Starside Movers. You can switch them off any time in the game’s Settings or with the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Anonymous counts such as “someone opened the game” or “a round finished”, and anonymous error reports so we can fix bugs. These aren’t linked to your name or email.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your email or share it with advertisers.
  • Leaderboards show your mover name only, never your email.

Emails and unsubscribing

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link that works with one tap. If you said yes to news, we may send one email to confirm it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your sign-up while the game is running. To have it deleted, {{CONTACT}}.

Storage on your device

The game remembers your mover, settings and sound preference in your browser’s local storage so you don’t have to sign in every time.
