How to play
  • Grab boxes in the house and carry them to the load zone at the truck.
  • On the ramp, move to aim a column, then press action to drop it in. It stacks like Tetris.
  • Heavy boxes and furniture crush fragile boxes beneath them. Heavy on the bottom.
  • Hold action to pad-wrap couches and lamps, or take apart desks and tables.
  • Couches, beds, desks and tables are two-mover lifts. Call for help if you need it.
  • Heard a crunch? Call out the culprit. Right call: they get a strike. Wrong call: you do.
