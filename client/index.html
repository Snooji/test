How to play
  • Find the pieces in the house and carry them to the load zone by the truck's ramp. Walls hide the next room, so explore.
  • In the truck, aim where the piece goes, then place it. Rotate turns it, Flip tips it onto another side.
  • Boxes look alike: a broken glass mark means fragile, an anvil means heavy. Heavy on the bottom, fragile on top.
  • Hold the action to pad-wrap a couch or lamp, or to take a desk or table apart.
  • Couches, beds, desks and tables are two-mover lifts. Call for help, or use the furniture dolly (downstairs only). The two-wheel dolly stacks three boxes and can go up and down stairs.
  • Bigger houses have stairs: walk up a flight to the next floor. The minimap shows the floor you're on.
  • Carrying uses energy. Rush and you run out, get hurt and slow down; stand still to catch your breath.
  • Tap a lift someone else is carrying to start a 5-second tap battle for it. Taps count by how much energy you have left.
  • Heard a crunch? Call out the culprit. Right call: they get a strike. Wrong call: you do.
