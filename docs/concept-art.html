Starside Movers

A 1–4 player moving-day party game. Astronauts and aliens load a truck like a 3D puzzle, stack it the way a pro crew would, and hold a crew meeting whenever something goes crunch.

Level 1: Lunar Cul-de-Sac

A four-room house on the moon. Movers start on the truck's ramp, run in through the front door and carry everything back to the load zone. This map is drawn from the same coordinates the server uses for collision, so it matches the game exactly.

The truck: a 4 × 4 × 4 cargo grid

On the ramp, movement aims a column and the item drops to the lowest resting height, Tetris-style. Two-cell furniture can be rotated. The grid has 64 cells and the house holds 29 items, so there's room for mistakes. It's the mistakes that cost you.

Heavy on the bottom

Heavy boxes and furniture crush any fragile box they land on. The loader of the broken box loses its points.

Wrap it or scuff it

An unwrapped couch loads at half points. An unwrapped lamp shatters.

Call out

Breaks are anonymous. A correct call costs the culprit −15 and a strike. A wrong call costs the accuser.

Off the truck

Two strikes and you become a ghost. The winner is the top scorer still on the truck.

Inventory & points

Each item teaches one real moving rule. Two-mover furniture points are split between the carriers, and anyone who helps wrap or disassemble earns +5.
